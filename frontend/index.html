Nyquist Bandwidth Formula:

Max Data Rate = 2B · log₂(M)

Calculates theoretical bit capacity over a noiseless channel with bandwidth B.

Shannon Capacity Theorem:

C = B · log₂(1 + SNR)

Determines tight upper bound on transfer rate through a noisy channel.
